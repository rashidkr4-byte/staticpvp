In-game Rules

Please adhere strictly to our community guidelines to avoid punishment.

1 Keep chat clean of spam, flooding, toxicity, discrimination, and inappropriate content.

  • Do not attempt to duel or gather players by a specific gender or age in public chat.
  • Do not attempt to date players in public chat.

2 Do not use any disallowed modifications.

  • This includes macros and autoclickers. Double-binding keys for game actions is forbidden.
  • Using external programs (e.g., FilterKeys) to modify keyboard repeat rates is prohibited.
  • In general, if a mod lets you do something impossible in vanilla, it is likely disallowed.

3 Do not advertise.

  • Livestreams on Static Club are allowed via "live @username" messages only.
  • You are NOT allowed to publicly advertise groups, Minecraft servers, or SMPs.

4 Do not threaten the safety of other players.

Doxing, threatening, or harassing any individuals is completely unacceptable.

5-8 General Conduct

Do not exploit server performance, impersonate staff, use inappropriate skins/names, or evade punishments.
